T10 — artifact delivery adversarial checks (2026-08-26)

Synthetic tests reject:
  - absolute paths, traversal, dot/empty segments and backslash separators;
  - final symlinks through O_NOFOLLOW and intermediate symlink escapes through the opened
    descriptor target check;
  - non-regular/empty files and MIME declarations that disagree with magic bytes;
  - multipart, reversed, zero-suffix and out-of-bounds ranges (HTTP 416);
  - unauthenticated local requests before metadata lookup;
  - public/disabled mode before authentication or database access.

Failure responses do not include relative paths, absolute roots or internal exception details.
